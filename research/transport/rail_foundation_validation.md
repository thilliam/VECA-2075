# Rail foundation extraction validation

Source: Geoscience Australia Foundation Rail Infrastructure, Railway_Lines layer.

Extracted **28,039 line features** intersecting the broad EXP-001 envelope {'xmin': 138.5, 'ymin': -39.8, 'xmax': 154.2, 'ymax': -25.0}.

The source is a national aggregation of publicly available rail data and includes railway, siding and tram/light-rail line features grouped by operational status.

## Important limitations

- This is foundation geometry, not a statement of service frequency, capacity, gauge, ownership or freight importance.
- The extraction envelope is deliberately broad and is not a VECA preferred corridor.
- Usage/intensity must be layered separately from BITRE/NFDH/state/operator evidence.
- Historical/disused/proposed records must remain distinguishable from operational lines.

See `data/derived/transport/ga_rail_east_summary.csv` for service-attribute counts.
