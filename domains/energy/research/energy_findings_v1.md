# Energy findings v1

## E1 — Transmission status matters as much as renewable resource

**Classification:** Evidence + inference

AEMO's 2026 ISP separates committed/anticipated, actionable and future network projects. VECA should preserve those classes because a region with strong renewable resource but only future transmission is not equivalent to one already receiving committed reinforcement.

Examples relevant to the eastern system include HumeLink, Central-West Orana and New England network infrastructure, Sydney Ring North/South, VNI West, Brisbane Area reinforcement and future QNI Connect.

**Inference:** future settlement/industry analysis should measure inherited and committed transfer capability separately from theoretical renewable potential.

## E2 — New England is becoming a large planned energy node

**Classification:** Evidence

EnergyCo describes the New England REZ, centred around Armidale, as an initial 6 GW network over two stages with potential to increase to 8 GW. Current planning therefore creates an energy-system reason to study New England independently of its present population.

**Guardrail:** this is not evidence that Armidale/New England is an optimal VECA settlement location. Water, land, climate, industry, access and social licence remain separate questions.

## E3 — Central-West Orana demonstrates why one 'GW' field is inadequate

**Classification:** Evidence

Central-West Orana material distinguishes initial operating transfer capability, a higher transfer-capacity determination and a still higher aggregate generation/storage connection capacity.

**Inference:** VECA's energy model must carry at least `generation_capacity`, `storage_power`, `storage_energy`, `connection_capacity` and `transfer_capacity` as separate concepts where available.

## E4 — Victoria's future energy geography is scenario-sensitive

**Classification:** Evidence

The 2025 Victorian Transmission Plan models materially different amounts of new wind/solar by 2040 across scenarios. Its high-renewables scenario places much more development inside REZs than lower-build scenarios.

**Inference:** VECA should not treat today's generation map as destiny. Transmission reservations and REZ geography may matter more to 2075 settlement optionality than present generation alone.

## E5 — Generation/storage connection activity is moving very quickly

**Classification:** Evidence

AEMO's June 2026 Connections Scorecard reported a record 9.1 GW of generation and storage reaching full output during FY2025-26, while the broader connection pipeline remained very large.

**Inference:** the asset-level energy layer needs a refreshable source rather than a hand-maintained static list. AEMO's quarterly Generation Information dataset is the preferred backbone.

## EXP-001 position

Energy is now sufficiently structured to be **represented** in the inherited-system map through REZs and transmission status. It is not yet complete at asset level: existing generators, batteries, pumped hydro, substations and major loads still need systematic extraction from AEMO/state data.
