# Newcastle–Sydney HSR — procurement/package leads

**Date:** 10 September 2026  
**Status:** supporting research note. Primary HSRA/AusTender confirmation is required where stated below.

## Confirmed current ECI position

HSRA/Australian Government sources confirm that in August 2026 three consortia were shortlisted for **Area Package 1 (Berowra–Ourimbah)** ECI and three rail-system proponents were shortlisted for **TSSI** ECI. These are development competitions, not major construction awards.

## April 2026 EOI wrinkle: AP1 and AP3 were advertised together

Public tender mirrors for Australian Government ATM **10031970** reproduce an HSRA Expression of Interest titled **“Area Packages 1 and 3 (AP1/3)”**, published 21 April 2026 and closing 17 June 2026. The reproduced scope states:

- **AP1:** Ourimbah–Berowra, including tunnelling, track infrastructure, tunnel fit-out and design/construction of the Central Coast Station precinct/interchange.
- **AP3:** Berowra–Sydney Central, including tunnelling, track infrastructure and tunnel fit-out.

This matters because the later 17 August 2026 government shortlist announcement names only **AP1**. VECA should therefore not infer that AP3 has reached the same shortlist/ECI status without a primary current award/shortlist notice. The procurement history should record **EOI called jointly for AP1/AP3; current confirmed ECI shortlist publicly announced for AP1**.

Primary AusTender documentation, including addenda and the complete EOI document suite, remains a priority retrieval target.

## Indicative whole-program package architecture — not yet primary-verified in this pass

A March 2026 legal/industry analysis reproducing and discussing the HSRA Business Case delivery strategy describes a highly disaggregated package architecture and identifies the following geographic logic:

- Stage 1A: Newcastle/Broadmeadow – Lake Macquarie – Central Coast;
- Stage 1B: Central Coast – Sydney Central;
- Stage 1C: Sydney Central – Parramatta – Western Sydney International;
- AP4: Cardiff–Broadmeadow plus Newcastle station;
- AP2: Ourimbah–Cardiff plus Lake Macquarie station;
- AP1: Berowra–Ourimbah plus Central Coast station;
- AP3: Berowra–Central;
- Sydney Central station as a separate package;
- AP5: Central–Parramatta;
- Parramatta station separately;
- AP6: Parramatta–WSI;
- system-wide TSSI and advanced-manufacturing packages.

The same analysis says the Business Case originally identified up to 19 contract packages and notes that subsequent adviser/procurement documents already appear to rationalise some packaging. This is **secondary interpretation of primary HSRA material** and is retained as a retrieval lead, not promoted to verified project truth until the Business Case/industry briefing package diagram is directly indexed.

## Why this matters for changeability

Package boundaries are themselves creating path dependency. AP1 already anchors Berowra–Ourimbah and a Central Coast station/interchange into live ECI work, while AP3 has been exposed to the market through the April EOI. Even though HSRA says the alignment is being optimised/finalised, a proposal requiring wholesale movement of those package endpoints would now face materially more resistance than an optimisation inside or between the established package interfaces.

Conversely, the fact that packaging has already changed/refined between Business Case concepts, adviser procurements and current ECI demonstrates that **delivery architecture is not immutable** during Development Phase.
