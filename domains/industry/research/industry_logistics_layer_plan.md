# EXP-001 industry and logistics layer plan

## Purpose

Identify inherited and committed economic nodes whose strategic importance is not captured by resident population alone.

The layer should distinguish:

- ports and airport freight gateways;
- intermodal terminals and inland ports;
- established industrial precincts;
- government-serviced activation precincts;
- advanced manufacturing / defence / food-processing clusters;
- major logistics/distribution investments;
- data-centre and large flexible-load clusters where evidenced;
- recent committed private capital.

## Guardrails

- A master-planned precinct is not the same as an operating industry cluster.
- Proposed private investment remains separate from completed/contracted investment.
- Jobs targets are not current employment.
- Freight accessibility is measured as network position, not simply distance to a motorway or railway.
- Industrial land with no water/energy/freight capacity does not receive automatic strategic value.

## Initial question

Where are governments and firms already creating non-CBD economic gravity that could plausibly support a larger regional settlement system by 2075?
