# Climate and hazard findings v1

## C1 — Heat increase is a near-universal east-coast pressure, not a differentiator by itself

**Classification:** Evidence + inference

CSIRO/BOM projections show warming across Australia through the 2050/2070 horizons. VECA therefore should not award a region points merely for being cooler today; it should compare future absolute heat, extreme-heat frequency, night-time cooling and adaptation burden.

## C2 — Southern water stress deserves explicit treatment

**Classification:** Evidence

CSIRO/BOM projects increasing potential evapotranspiration with high confidence, increased time in drought across southern Australia, and decreasing soil moisture from mid-century particularly in southern regions. Southern mainland winter/spring rainfall is projected to decline with high confidence.

**Inference:** Melbourne/Victoria, Riverina and southern inland candidates require future water-yield analysis rather than relying on current storage/river infrastructure alone.

## C3 — Extreme rainfall and drought can worsen simultaneously

**Classification:** Evidence

National projections indicate more intense extreme-rainfall events while drought/time-in-drought and evaporative demand also increase in relevant regions.

**Inference:** 'drier future' must not be translated into lower flood risk. Urban-drainage, riverine-flood and drought-security layers remain separate.

## C4 — Fire-weather pressure rises across southern and eastern Australia

**Classification:** Evidence

CSIRO/BOM projects harsher fire weather across southern and eastern Australia with high confidence.

**Inference:** bushfire exposure is likely to be one of the strongest spatial filters for forest-fringe and mountain/tableland settlement options, but must be evaluated locally with vegetation, topography, evacuation and defensible-space data.

## C5 — National flood data has a coverage-age problem

**Classification:** Evidence

Geoscience Australia's AFRIP is a catalogue of flood studies/maps contributed up to 2018, not a current national design-flood surface.

**Inference:** VECA should use AFRIP to discover studies and gaps, then obtain current state/local flood studies for serious candidate analysis. Historical water-observation products can supplement but not replace hydraulic modelling.
