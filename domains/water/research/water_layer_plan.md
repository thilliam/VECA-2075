# EXP-001 water layer plan

## Purpose

Build a coherent inherited-water-system layer for eastern Australia before any settlement suitability scoring.

Water is not represented by dam capacity alone. Each functional water system should distinguish:

- source catchments and major storages;
- river/regulation dependence;
- groundwater dependence;
- treatment capacity where material;
- bulk transfer pipelines/interconnectors;
- desalination and purified recycled water;
- current/committed augmentation;
- climate dependence and drought operating rules;
- major industrial/agricultural competition where evidenced.

## Analytical unit

Use **functional water systems** first, then component assets. A city may be resilient because several modest sources are interconnected, while a very large reservoir may be less useful to another region because transfer infrastructure does not exist.

## Questions for VECA

- Which regions already have diversified water sources?
- Where can water move between catchments or cities through existing bulk infrastructure?
- Which systems remain highly rainfall-dependent?
- Where is climate-independent supply already built or planned?
- Where can recycled water substitute for potable industrial demand?
- Which regional centres sit beside large regulated river systems but are constrained by treatment, licence or ecological limits rather than raw physical water?
- Which apparent water advantages disappear when pumping energy, elevation and pipeline distance are included?

## Guardrails

- Storage volume is not annual sustainable yield.
- Current dam percentage is an operating-state observation, not long-run security.
- River proximity is not a water entitlement.
- Groundwater is not assumed renewable at extraction rate.
- Desalination is not treated as making all coastal locations equivalent; energy, capital, brine disposal and transfer infrastructure remain relevant.
- Agricultural/environmental water is not assumed freely transferable to urban use.

## EXP-001 completion criterion

Water is represented when the major eastern functional systems and candidate-region systems have sourced descriptions of supply type, major assets, diversification/transfer capability and known augmentation, with explicit gaps where sustainable-yield data is not comparable.
