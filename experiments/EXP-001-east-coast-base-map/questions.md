# EXP-001 Research Questions

## Primary question

What does the inherited eastern Australian spatial system look like when current population, major transport, recent and committed capital investment, energy, water, industry and logistics are viewed together?

## Layer questions

- Where is population currently concentrated, and where are meaningful secondary population centres?
- Which major passenger, freight, road, airport, port and intermodal assets structure the corridor today?
- Where has major public and private infrastructure capital been deployed since roughly 2015?
- Which regions are receiving genuinely committed new capacity?
- Which apparent future projects are only planned or proposed?
- Where do renewable generation, REZs, storage and major transmission create emerging energy geography?
- What major water assets and known water-security constraints shape the corridor?
- Where are major industrial, logistics and port clusters?

## Cross-layer questions

- Where are multiple forms of enabling infrastructure accumulating in the same region?
- Where does large spending primarily appear to remediate congestion or legacy constraints rather than create new strategic capacity?
- Which regions appear underrepresented by current infrastructure relative to their geographic or resource position?
- Which spatial patterns are surprising enough to justify a dedicated follow-on experiment?
- Which evidence gaps would materially distort later settlement analysis if left unresolved?

These questions guide observation only. EXP-001 does not answer where 10 million additional residents should live.
