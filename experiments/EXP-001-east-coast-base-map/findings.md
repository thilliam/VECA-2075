# EXP-001 Findings

Research is active. Findings below remain deliberately narrow and evidence-classified.

## Population growth remains strongly metropolitan-fringe weighted

**Classification:** Evidence  
**Claim:** The largest SA2 population increases in 2024–25 were concentrated heavily on the outer edges of major metropolitan areas. In eastern Australia, examples include Rockbank - Mount Cottrell, Mickleham - Yuroke and Fraser Rise - Plumpton in Melbourne, Box Hill - Nelson in Sydney, and Ripley in Ipswich / Greater Brisbane.  
**Supporting source IDs:** `SRC-ABS-REGPOP-2025`  
**Geographic scope:** eastern Australian metropolitan regions  
**Time/data period:** 2024–25; ERP at 30 June 2025  
**Confidence / limitations:** High confidence as a description of one-year population change. It does not establish long-run settlement desirability or infrastructure efficiency.  
**Implication for next research:** The inherited growth system is still extending metropolitan edges rapidly. Later VECA comparison should distinguish this observed momentum from evidence that metropolitan-fringe expansion is optimal.

## Regional eastern Australia is also adding population

**Classification:** Evidence  
**Claim:** In 2024–25, population outside Greater Brisbane increased by about 39,700 people (1.4%), outside Greater Sydney by about 25,200 (0.9%), and outside Greater Melbourne by about 17,900 (1.1%). ABS also identifies Caloundra West - Baringa as the largest absolute-growth SA2 outside Australian capital cities and Googong, immediately outside the ACT, as the highest-growth-rate SA2 outside the capitals.  
**Supporting source IDs:** `SRC-ABS-REGPOP-2025`  
**Geographic scope:** Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria outside their GCCSAs  
**Time/data period:** 2024–25  
**Confidence / limitations:** High confidence for the stated ABS aggregates. "Rest of state" is far too coarse to identify which regional systems are structurally strong.  
**Implication for next research:** The full SA2 layer is required before making any claim about regional corridors or candidate centres.

## Current population geography can be extracted without cross-version polygon joining

**Classification:** Evidence  
**Claim:** The ABS `SA2 Regional Population 2025` feature service combines ASGS Edition 3 SA2 polygon geometry with ERP for 2001–2025, 2024–25 change, area, density and recent migration components in the same authoritative feature layer.  
**Supporting source IDs:** `SRC-ABS-SA2-ERP-2025`  
**Geographic scope:** Australia  
**Time/data period:** ERP 2001–2025; geography ASGS Edition 3  
**Confidence / limitations:** High. ASGS Edition 4 is newer, but adopting it now would require a concordance and offers no immediate benefit to EXP-001's inherited-system baseline.  
**Implication for next research:** Keep the first population map native to Edition 3 and defer geography migration until a later analytical need justifies it.
